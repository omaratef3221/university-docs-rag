## Degree Overview

The Bachelor of Law program in English, provides students with a comprehensive, advanced, and strong foundation in the field of law, enabling them to understand legal principles, systems, and practices. The program offers a qualitative education of high international standards and quality, in accordance with the latest international standards and practices adopted in the field of law by prestigious international universities. Students enrolled in the Bachelor of Law program in English, receive a basic knowledge of law, followed by an in-depth study of its branches and modern legal specializations, such as civil law, commercial law, arbitration, constitutional law, administrative law, penal law, civil and criminal procedure law, as well as mock trials and other specializations. This is within a comprehensive, high-quality education that combines theoretical study with practical experience. This education equips students with sound critical thinking skills and the ability to deduce, analyze, and discuss. This qualifies them to provide legal consultations, pleadings, draft memoranda, arbitration proceedings, and other related skills. Furthermore, students are equipped with the skills to understand legal logic, interpret laws, correctly apply legal rules, apply scientific research methodology, and analyze and comment on judicial rulings. All this and more benefits undergraduate law students at the University of Sharjah, which provides a unique and promising educational environment that links both theoretical and practical aspects of the educational process with modern technology, leveraging technological advancements to keep pace with legislative developments within a purposeful and sustainable vision.

#### Study Plan for Law in English

## What You Will Learn

- Identify the core principles of the Emirati legal system and its substantive and procedural rules.
- Critically analyze legal concepts and problems in their historical and contemporary context, formulate potential solutions and defend conclusions by evaluating relevant legal sources, ideas and evidence.
- Integrate knowledge and techniques associated with the discipline of law to effectively engage with the evolving nature of the discipline, legal profession and legal practice.
- Synthesize and manage information through effective legal research strategies and responsible use of appropriate resources, tools, digital and other media.
- Communicate orally and in writing through the appropriate use of media, technology and modes of communication with lawyers, clients and others.
- Engage in self-reflection and, within a coherent ethical framework, recognize different perspectives and roles in society, and make decisions consistent with professional responsibilities.
- Perform assigned legal tasks effectively and independently, specifically by exhibiting an ability to learn independently and manage time.
- Participate in cooperative groups to contribute actively and fairly in completing the assigned tasks and activities to meet team roles.
- Work effectively as a member of a team and apply leadership, self-learning and time management skills to meet tasks and projected goals.
