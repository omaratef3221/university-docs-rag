## What You Will Learn

SREE department provides our graduate engineers with top-rated, professional education in the field of sustainable and renewable energy engineering. The program is intended to be a leader in providing theoretical knowledge and practices required for the design, development, implementation, and improvement of integrated sustainable energy systems that include people, materials, information, and equipment required to generate, store, and distribute energy from sustainable and renewable sources.

## Career Path

SREE graduates have opportunities in designing, testing, developing, manufacturing, and operating sustainable and renewable energy systems, as well as in broader engineering environments. Given the national and international energy outlooks, there is substantial demand for Renewable Energy Engineers, particularly in the UAE and Gulf Countries. Our graduates are poised to contribute across the spectrum of SREE, encompassing roles such as energy auditors, renewable energy system integrators for residential and commercial properties, manufacturing engineers for component and subsystem manufacturers, designers for components and subsystems, renewable energy inspectors, planners, and other positions within the energy sector.
