## Accreditation

The Bachelor of Science programs in Architectural Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Industrial Engineering and Engineering Management, Mechanical Engineering, Nuclear Engineering, and Sustainable and Renewable Energy Engineering are accredited by the Engineering Accreditation Commission (EAC) of ABET, https://www.abet.org.
