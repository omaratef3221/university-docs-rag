## La Roca (The Museum of Materials)

The design approach primarily focuses on enhancing user experience through interactive elements and sensory experiences within the building itself. It aims to relate the building to the urban fabric of Barcelona, promoting knowledge, understanding, and appreciation of the role of materials in construction and architecture. The design takes visitors on a journey through the various stages of building materials, exploring material expression. This concept culminates in La Roca (The Museum of Materials), a sculpture from nature symbolizing the origin of everything.
