## SOTI (Sharjah Oasis for Technology and Innovation)

As the theme for SOTI is inspired by Islamic and Emirati architecture, we decided that the concept behind our urban plan should be to use a letter only found in the Arabic Language: ض. Then we developed the urban plan and divided the letter into crescents, a well-known symbol in Islamic architecture.
