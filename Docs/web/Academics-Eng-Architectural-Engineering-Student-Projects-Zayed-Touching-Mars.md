## Zayed Touching Mars

Zayed Touching Mars will eliminate any barriers to experiencing Mars, enabling students, emissaries, and tourists to live and experience what will happen in 50 years time. The aim is to educate, entertain, and exceed the expectations that people have for the desired Martian environment. This project will feature a simulation center and a simulation camp.
