## COP28 Participation

Students from the Department of Sustainable and Renewable Energy Engineering at the University of Sharjah participated in COP28 UAE with cutting-edge presentations. Those presentations were under the supervision of Dr. Ali Radwan, Dr. Ammar A. T. Alkhalidi, Prof. Ibrahim AlSharkawy, Prof. Abdul Hai Alalami, and Prof. Chaouki Ghenai.
