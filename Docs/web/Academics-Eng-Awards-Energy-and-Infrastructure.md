## Energy and Infrastructure

Under the patronage of His Excellency Suhail bin Muhammad Al Mazrouei, Minister of Energy and Infrastructure, several students and faculty members from the Department of Sustainable and Renewable Energy Engineering received multiple awards in different categories: Research and Innovation Award for Individual Researchers (Award Category: Better Integration of Urban Planning)1st Place: Dr. Zafar Said UAE Innovates Award (Innovative Projects in the Ministry's Innovation Week)Dr. Abrar Inayat and his team: Reem Younis, Mahmoud Abdalla Alamiri, Nada Nouichi, Maryam Hassan Jaffar Hassan Ahli, Sara Mamoun Mohamed Rafeea Ibrahim Almarzooqi Prof. Abdul Hai Al-Alami and his team: Maitha Mohammad Khalfan Huwaireb Almheiri, Sara Abdallah Baha Uddin Numan, Yara Mahmoud TillawiIn the same event, both Dr. Abrar Inayat and Dr. Ahmed Galal Abokhalil were recognized for their contributions as judges for the awards.
