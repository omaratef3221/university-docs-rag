## University of Sharjah Annual Faculty Incentive Award – Academic Year 2024/2025

We are proud to announce that five faculty members from the College of Engineering have received prestigious awards during the 2024/2025 academic year. The College of Engineering secured the top three positions in the University of Sharjah Annual Faculty Incentive Award under the Scientific Research category, showcasing the college’s exceptional contributions to research. Additionally, one faculty member was recognized in the Teaching category, and another received the distinguished Sharjah Islamic Bank Award for Scientific Research. University of Sharjah Annual Faculty Incentive Award – Academic Year 2024/2025 Scientific Research Category • Prof. Ramesh Bansal • Dr. Ali Mohammed Radwan • Prof. Ammar A. T. Alkhalidi Teaching Category • Dr. Ahmed Galal Abokhalil Sharjah Islamic Bank Award for Scientific Research – Academic Year 2024/2025 Faculty Category • Dr. Sourjya Bhattacharjee We extend our heartfelt congratulations to all the awardees for their dedication, excellence, and contributions to academic and scientific advancement.
