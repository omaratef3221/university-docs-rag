## Advanced Geotechnical and Mechanical Characterization of Hydraulically Dredged Gravelly Soils for UAE Land Reclamation

Venturing into the domain of geotechnical engineering, researchers undertake an Advanced Characterization endeavor to unravel the complexities of Hydraulically Dredged Gravelly Soils, crucial for UAE Land Reclamation projects. Employing sophisticated geotechnical and mechanical analyses, they aim to comprehensively understand the unique properties and behavior of these soils, thereby facilitating informed decision-making in land development endeavors.
