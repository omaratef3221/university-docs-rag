## Assessing Land Surface Temperature for Sustainable Urban Planning in the UAE

Urban growth increases land surface temperature (LST), impacting sustainability and public comfort. In arid cities like Sharjah, understanding LST patterns is key for climate-resilient planning. Traditional ground measurements offer high detail but limited coverage, while remote sensing covers broader areas with lower frequency.
