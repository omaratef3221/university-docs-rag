## Devising and Automating Process of Using Unmanned Aerial Vehicle & Deep Learning Algorithms for Traffic & Safety Analysis at Multilane Roundabout

Evaluating roundabout performance requires traffic volumes, vehicle classifications, and trajectories, butcollecting these parameters is difficult due to complex maneuvers and conflict areas. UAV-mounted camerasoffer a top-view perspective that overcomes many limitations of ground-based data collection.
