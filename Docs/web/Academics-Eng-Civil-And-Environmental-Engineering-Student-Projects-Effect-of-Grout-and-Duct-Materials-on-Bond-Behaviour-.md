## Effect of Grout and Duct Materials on Bond Behaviour of Prestressed Strands Subjected to Accelerated Corrosion and Implication on Structural Response

Exploring the intricacies of Prestressed Concrete Structures, researchers investigate the Impact of Grout and Duct Materials on Bond Behavior under conditions of Accelerated Corrosion. By elucidating the complex interplay between material properties and environmental factors, they aim to unravel the implications on structural response, thereby informing strategies for mitigating corrosion-induced deterioration in prestressed concrete systems.
