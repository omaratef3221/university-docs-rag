## Experimental Study on the Strength and Bond Parameters of Fiber-Reinforced Polymer (FRP) Concrete Subjected to Environmental Conditioning

In another endeavor, researchers conduct an Experimental Study focused on elucidating the Strength and Bond Parameters intrinsic to Fiber-Reinforced Polymer (FRP) Concrete, under the rigors of Environmental Conditioning. Through systematic testing and analysis, they seek to discern the influence of environmental factors on the mechanical properties and durability of FRP concrete, thus informing strategies for enhanced material performance in real-world applications.
