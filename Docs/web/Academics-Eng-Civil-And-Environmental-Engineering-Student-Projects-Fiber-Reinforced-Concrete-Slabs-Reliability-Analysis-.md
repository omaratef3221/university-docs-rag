## Fiber-Reinforced Concrete Slabs: Reliability Analysis and Calibration of Strength Reduction Factors

In a quest for structural reliability, researchers conduct Reliability Analysis and Calibration studies focusing on Fiber-Reinforced Concrete Slabs. Through meticulous analysis of experimental data and probabilistic modeling, they aim to quantify the reliability and performance of fiber-reinforced concrete systems, thereby informing design practices and standards for enhanced structural safety.
