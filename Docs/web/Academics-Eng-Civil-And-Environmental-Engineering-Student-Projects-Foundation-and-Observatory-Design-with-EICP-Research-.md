## Foundation and Observatory Design with EICP Research Soil Enhancement

By understanding the characteristics of the soil,geotechnical engineers help design variousstructures, including roads, railways, retaining walls,and building foundations, and work to minimize theimpact of natural disasters like earthquakes,landslides, and rock falls. This senior design projectcombined theoretical knowledge, field observation,laboratory experimentation, and advanced modelingtools to achieve a comprehensive study ingeotechnical and structural engineering.
