## Investigation of Thermo-mechanical Behavior of Sand-Concrete Interfaces for Shallow Geothermal Pile Applications

Innovating in the realm of geotechnical engineering, researchers delve into the Thermo-mechanical Behavior of Sand-Concrete Interfaces, particularly relevant for Shallow Geothermal Pile Applications. Through a comprehensive investigation spanning experimental testing and numerical modeling, they seek to enhance understanding of interface interactions under thermal loading, thereby optimizing the design and performance of geothermal pile systems.
