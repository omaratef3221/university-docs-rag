## Modelling the shear strength of FRP Strengthened RC Beams Using Artificial Neural Networks

Employing Artificial Intelligence techniques, researchers undertake Modeling efforts to predict the Shear Strength of FRP Strengthened RC Beams. Leveraging vast datasets and advanced algorithms, they aim to develop accurate predictive models capable of capturing the complex mechanical behavior of FRP-strengthened structures, thus facilitating efficient and reliable structural design.
