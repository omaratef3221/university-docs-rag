## Probabilistic Assessment of ACI 318 Minimum Thickness Requirements for Two Way Reinforced Concrete Slabs

Delving into the realm of structural engineering, researchers embark on a thorough examination of the Probabilistic Assessment concerning the Minimum Thickness Requirements stipulated in the ACI 318 code for Two-Way Reinforced Concrete Slabs. By employing advanced probabilistic techniques, they aim to unravel the nuances and intricacies inherent in these requirements, offering insights into the reliability and safety margins crucial for robust structural designs.
