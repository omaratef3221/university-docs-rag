## Punching Shear Strength of Reinforced Concrete Slabs with Macro Synthetic Fibers

In an in-depth exploration, researchers scrutinize the Punching Shear Strength of Reinforced Concrete Slabs incorporating Macro Synthetic Fibers. Through meticulous experimentation and analysis, they aim to elucidate the impact of these fibers on the slab's ability to withstand punching shear forces. By examining the interaction between the fibers and concrete matrix under varying conditions, they seek to enhance understanding and offer insights for optimizing the design and construction of reinforced concrete structures, particularly in scenarios prone to punching shear failure.
