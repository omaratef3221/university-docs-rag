## Quantifying the Impacts of Connected and Autonomous Vehicles on Traffic Operation and GHG Emissions through Micro-Simulation

Within the domain of transportation engineering, meticulous attention is directed towards understanding the multifaceted impacts of Connected and Autonomous Vehicles (CAVs) on both traffic operation and greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions. Employing micro-simulation methodologies, researchers meticulously quantify the intricate interplay between CAV deployment, traffic dynamics, and environmental sustainability, thus shedding light on potential avenues for optimizing transportation systems of the future.
