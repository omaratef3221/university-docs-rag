## Water Demand Forecasting for Sustainable Design of Water Supply System

Embracing the principles of sustainable urban development, researchers delve into the intricacies of Water Demand Forecasting to inform the design of a resilient Water Supply System. By leveraging predictive modeling techniques and data analytics, they seek to anticipate future water consumption patterns, thereby enabling the implementation of proactive measures to ensure the reliability and sustainability of water supply infrastructure in the face of evolving societal needs and environmental challenges.
