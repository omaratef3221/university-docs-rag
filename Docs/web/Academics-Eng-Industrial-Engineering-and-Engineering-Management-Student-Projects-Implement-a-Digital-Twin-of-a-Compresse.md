## Design and Implement a Digital Twin of a Compressed Air Energy Storage System for Real Time Optimization

This project aims to design and implement Digital Twin (DT) technology to improve a Compressed Air Energy Storage (CAES) system. This is to solve problems in modern energy systems, specially in making energy storage more efficient. The goal is to build a digital twin for compressed systems by monitoring data in real-time and detecting faults. A real twin has been created and tested on a real compressed system.
