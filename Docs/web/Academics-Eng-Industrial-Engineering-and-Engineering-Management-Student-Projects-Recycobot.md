## Recycobot

Recycobot, an innovative smart robot, addresses the global plastic and paper waste problem by autonomously collecting and recycling waste. Equipped with advanced sensors, it efficiently navigates and collects waste while minimizing unnecessary trips. This report provides insights into Recycobot's design, sensors, capabilities, and operational code.
