## Revamping of a Fuel Cell System from Industry 2.0 to Industry 4.0: From Business Intelligence to Business Analytics

This project upgraded an outdated inert fuel cell system to Industry 4.0 standards, using modern digital technologies like business analytics and real-time monitoring. The renovation involved hardware refurbishment and integration of tools like Power BI, enhancing operational efficiency and maintenance capabilities. This demonstrates the feasibility of modernizing legacy systems to align with global sustainability goals.
