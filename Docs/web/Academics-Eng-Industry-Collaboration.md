## Industry Collaboration

At the University of Sharjah (UoS), we offer nine of the most respected undergraduate engineering programs in the country. Our students thrive in an environment that blends technical expertise with a liberal arts foundation, promoting close interactions with faculty and extensive hands-on learning experiences. We welcome collaboration opportunities with companies, non-profits, schools, and individuals to engage in projects that leverage our students' skills to drive innovation within your organization, address challenges with fresh perspectives, meet recruitment goals by connecting with talented students, and enhance our students' education through practical, real-world experiences.

Faculty and Student Projects

At the College of Engineering, faculty and students actively collaborate with industry to bridge academic knowledge and practical application. These partnerships enable students to work on real-world projects, enhancing their skills and making them industry-ready. By engaging with industry leaders, we ensure our curriculum remains relevant and innovative.

The approach we take ensures students are encouraged to:

- Engage in innovative research projects that investigate the rapidly-changing global landscape of engineering and technology. Students that participate in these programs gain priceless knowledge and abilities that will influence engineering and technological advancement in the future.
- Question accepted wisdom by using critical thinking, creative problem-solving, and entrepreneurial endeavors to create an atmosphere of creative inquiry and innovation in the field of engineering and technology.
- Accept a range of viewpoints and career paths to spur creativity and advance the development of novel answers to challenging problems facing the industry.
- Work in tandem with local, regional, and international communities to develop long-term strategies that will increase the availability of engineering education and technology.
- Engage in community-driven projects and put in place efficient programs to advance engineering technological development endeavors throughout time.
The College of Engineering has successfully completed numerous projects in collaboration with industry partners, showcasing our commitment to practical and impactful education. These projects span various engineering disciplines, providing students with hands-on experience and innovative solutions to real-world challenges. Collaborate With Us

We invite you to collaborate with us at the College of Engineering. By partnering with us, you can:

- Tap into our pool of talented students and leverage faculty expertise to drive innovation in your organization.
- Engage in meaningful projects that tackle real-world challenges and opportunities.
- Expand your network and connect with like-minded individuals who share your passion for making a difference.
