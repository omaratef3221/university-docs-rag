## Sunflower-Inspired Urban Design Sets New Standard for Solar Equity

A sunflower-inspired city design from the University of Sharjah sets a new benchmark for solar equity in urban planning. This nature-based layout ensures consistent sunlight access, surpassing grid and radial forms in rooftop and facade exposure.
