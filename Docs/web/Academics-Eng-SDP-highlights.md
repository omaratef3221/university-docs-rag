## College of Engineering Senior Design Projects Exhibition- 2025

The College of Engineering at the University of Sharjah successfully hosted its Annual Senior Design Projects Exhibition for the academic year 2024- 2025. The event showcased over 50 innovative projects developed by senior students across all engineering departments, reflecting creativity, technical excellence, and a strong focus on sustainability, smart technologies, and community service.

Attended by the Chancellor, faculty members, and industry professionals, the exhibition featured architectural models, interactive presentations, and advanced engineering solutions. Highlights included smart agricultural systems, 3D-printed structures, renewable energy innovations, and AI-powered technologies.

The exhibition celebrated students’ achievements and emphasized the university’s commitment to fostering innovation, real-world problem-solving, and preparing graduates for impactful careers in engineering and technology.
