## Advanced Atmospheric Water Harvesting for Sustainable Agriculture in Arid Climates

Our project, "Advanced Atmospheric Water Harvesting for Sustainable Agriculture in Arid Climates," belongs to the Department of Sustainable and Renewable Energy Engineering. It addresses water scarcity by using solar-powered silica gel systems to harvest water from air. The project benefits society by enhancing water security and supporting agriculture. It can help farmers, remote communities, and government water initiatives.
