## Design and Performance Analysis of a Solar Charging Station for an Integrated Battery/Hydrogen Fuel Cell Electric Golf Cart

Our project, Design and Performance Analysis of a Solar Charging Station for an Integrated Battery/Hydrogen Fuel Cell Electric Golf Cart, specializes in Sustainable and Renewable Energy Engineering (SREE). It addresses the global need for sustainable transportation solutions by integrating solar PV panels, batteries, and hydrogen fuel cells into a stand-alone EV charging station. This innovation benefits society by reducing emissions and energy waste. Potential beneficiaries include golf clubs, universities, and eco-resorts. The project is supported by the University of Sharjah.
