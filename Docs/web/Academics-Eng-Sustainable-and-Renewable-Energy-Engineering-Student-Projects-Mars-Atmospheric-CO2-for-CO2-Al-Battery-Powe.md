## Mars Atmospheric CO2 for CO2/Al-Battery-Powered PV Cleaning

Global warming now is of great concern because of its potential to cause significant and long-lasting impacts on our planet, its ecosystem, and human societies. Global warming main cause is the greenhouse effect which includes a high percentage of carbon dioxide (CO2). Since CO2 emissions from fossil fuels and land-use are increasing, renewable energy was introduced. Our country highly encourages the use of renewables as the UAE 2050 Energy Strategy states. This encouraged us, three ambitious students of the new generation of our society, to partake in this field. Energy storage is a crucial factor for any energy generation system. We got more familiar with batteries and came across the O2-Assisted Al/CO2 battery, which was suitable for our projects and its application. Moreover, we anticipate that this battery design can be applied equally for extra-terrestrial applications for CO2 capture purposes as well as for superior planets where CO2 remains an integral part of its atmosphere.
