## Why Engineering?

Welcome to the College of Engineering at UoS, where excellence and innovation have intersected since 1980. Discover our nine nationally and internationally recognized bachelor's degrees, as well as advanced MSc and PhD programs across diverse engineering disciplines, all accredited by the Accreditation Board for Engineering and Technology (ABET). Our graduates are industry-ready, thanks to a curriculum designed to meet global standards.

Our unique undergraduate engineering programs, namely Architecture Engineering, Civil and Environmental Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Industrial Engineering and Engineering Management, Mechanical Engineering, Nuclear Engineering, Chemical and Water Desalination Engineering, Sustainable and Renewable Energy Engineering, and the most recent one, Mechatronic and Robotics Engineering, set the standard in the region. Experience our state-of-the-art facilities and smart technologies on the new campus, fostering a dynamic learning and research environment. Through industry partnerships, internships, and seminars, students gain practical insights that keep the curriculum relevant. Utilizing industry-standard software and specialized packages, students engage in hands-on learning and project-based experiences, preparing them for real-world success.
