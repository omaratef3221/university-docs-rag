## Postponing Admission for Undergraduates

- Admitted students should enroll at the beginning of every semester according to the registration schedule announced in the University’s academic calendar.
- New students are allowed to postpone their admission for a maximum of one
- Students must pay the required fees and submit a deferment form to the Admissions Office before classes Once that is done, the College concerned shall be duly notified. If the student does not register the following semester, his/her admission shall be cancelled.
- Students who do not enroll for the semester in which they were admitted or do not process a request for postponement of admission will be denied admission to the University and will not have the right to a refund of the seat reservation fees
- Admission postpone is not allowed in Colleges of Medicine and Dental Medicine
Students who would like to postpone their admission, please apply for the service through the following link: Postpone Admission Request
