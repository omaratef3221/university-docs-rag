## Housing Fees “Academic Year 2026/2027”

The University Student Housing is committed to providing a safe, well-maintained, and comfortable living environment for all residents. In line with this commitment and with the on-going efforts to elevate the housing services, the University is planning a series of facility and operational enhancements including the introduction of enhanced housekeeping services. In support of these improvements and as a response to the growing demand for on-campus housing, housing fees will be subject to a 20% increase (towards 10% housing fees and 10% housekeeping services), effective from Academic Year 2025/26.
