## Living Expenses

When planning to study at the University of Sharjah, it is essential to budget carefully for your living expenses. Below is a detailed breakdown of the key costs you may need to consider:

#### Accommodation

Whether you choose to rent an off-campus apartment or opt for on-campus housing, accommodation will significantly impact your budget. Costs will vary depending on factors such as location, available facilities, and whether you select private or shared living arrangements.

#### Medical Expenses

Health insurance is mandatory. While your insurance may cover a portion of your medical costs, it is advisable to set aside funds for non-covered expenses such as prescription medications, over-the-counter drugs, and doctor visits.

#### Transportation

Sharjah offers public transportation options, including taxis and buses. Consider the daily commuting costs to and from the University. If you plan to drive, don’t forget to account for parking fees, fuel, and vehicle maintenance.

#### University Expenses

Beyond tuition, other academic-related expenses include textbooks, stationery, lab fees, and any specialized equipment required for your course. Be sure to include these in your budget planning.

#### Leisure Activities

Balance your academic responsibilities with personal time. Set aside funds for leisure activities such as entertainment, dining out, hobbies, and exploring Sharjah and nearby attractions. The amount will depend on your lifestyle and interests.

#### Mobile Phone and Internet

Reliable communication is vital. Be sure to budget for mobile phone plans and internet services. Both postpaid and prepaid plans offer a range of data packages, so choose one that suits your needs.

#### Tips for Budgeting

- Monitor your spending: Track your expenses to identify areas where you can save money.
- Prepare for emergencies: Set aside part of your budget for unexpected costs.
- Take advantage of student discounts: Many discounts are available for students. Taking advantage of these discounts will help you manage your finances more effectively.
