## Payment Method

### General Payment Method:

- Student Fees can be paid using the following methods:
The following payment options are available with no service charges.

- Online Payment through MyUDC (Tahseel Payment Gateway), which includes :
- Bank Transfer (Tahseel Gateway)
- Credit\Debit Card
- Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Samsung Pay
- Tabby & Tamara
- Cash Deposit through Sharjah Islamic Bank ATM or Tellers
- Cheque Deposit on campus
