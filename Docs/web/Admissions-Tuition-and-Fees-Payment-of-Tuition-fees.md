## Payment of Tuition Fees

### Flexible Payment Options:

As you prepare for the upcoming semester, we understand the importance of ensuring your financial obligations are met smoothly and efficiently, allowing you to focus on your academic pursuits.

Should you have any outstanding payments from previous semesters, rest assured, we offer convenient settlement options via cash, credit cards, or MY UDC.

Recognizing the complexity of managing tuition fees, we have devised a straightforward and adaptable payment plan. You now have the flexibility to divide your semester fees into two installments:

- The initial installment, constituting a minimum of 50% of the total amount, can be conveniently settled via Visa card or online payment.
- For the remaining 50%, you may opt for payment via two cheques, adhering to the guidelines outlined in our payment policy.
We provide a range of payment methods to suit your preferences and circumstances. Whether you prefer conventional methods such as cheque (drawn in favor of the University of Sharjah), or modern alternatives such as credit card, online payment (including Tabby and Tamara), or cash deposit through Sharjah Islamic Bank ATM, our commitment is to ensure your payment experience is seamless.

Type of Payment | Installment 1 | Installment 2 | Installment 3
Online Payment (First Payment) | 50% of Tuition fees | -
PDC (should be submitted before the final due date 03-09-2026 ) | - | 25% of tuition fees is due on 01/10/2026 | 25% of tuition fees due on 01/11/2026

** Kindly note that to secure any cheque, students are required to deposit the cheque amount and submit their request via the E-Service application at least 10 days prior to the cheque date. Thank you for your cooperation in facilitating efficient procedures.
