## Disability Support Services

At the University of Sharjah, we are committed to ensuring equal opportunities by providing the best possible support for students with disabilities. Suppose you are in one of the approved disability categories or experiencing challenges. In that case, we will be there to provide you with a range of support services throughout your university journey .

At the Disability Resource Centre, we are fully prepared to answer any questions you may have about disability support. An appointment can be arranged to discuss your personal needs. We suggest that you contact and register with the center as soon as possible to receive the best services .

You can also tell us about your condition before you visit the university by filling out a section of your application online via the following link .

This ensures our team can arrange support for you from the start. Rest assured, your condition details will not be disclosed without your consent.
