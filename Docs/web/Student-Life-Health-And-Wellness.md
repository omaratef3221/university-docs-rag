## Health and Wellness

Healthcare

Our 24-hour medical clinics and ambulance services are here for you, ensuring quick and dependable healthcare whenever you need it. Our campuses feature fully equipped medical clinics, ready to cater to the health needs of both male and female students. In addition to the state-of-the-art medical facilities, you can take advantage of our cutting-edge dental hospital services right here on campus, ensuring comprehensive healthcare support for all our students.

Entertainment & Social Life

Dive into the vibrant life at the University of Sharjah! From electrifying cultural celebrations such as UAE National Day to exciting student excursions to Spain and Turkey, we offer a plethora of engaging events and activities to enrich your university experience. These include theater productions written and directed by students, concerts, festivals, and awareness days like Biodiversity and Cancer Awareness days.

Psychological Counseling Office

At UoS, we prioritize mental health as much as physical health. Our Psychological Support and Counseling team is dedicated to fostering the well-being of our community. We provide comprehensive counseling services for students, staff, faculty, and parents, offering expert consultation from licensed mental health professionals. Our services include individual and group counseling sessions, psychoeducational workshops and seminars, crisis prevention, intervention, and consultation, psychological testing and clinical assessments, self-help resources, staff training, and referral services. We typically utilize a short-term, solution-focused approach spanning 8 to 10 sessions, with extended interventions available when necessary. Our goal is to help you navigate personal, family, and work-related concerns, ensuring you have the support and resources you need to thrive.

Disability Resource Center

Through our collaboration with Sharjah City for Humanitarian Services (SCHS), we have established the Disability Resource Center. At the University of Sharjah, we are committed to fostering an inclusive environment where all students can thrive. With tailored academic assistance, engaging extracurricular activities, and pathways to higher education, we empower students with disabilities to excel and contribute meaningfully to their communities.

Hair & Beauty Salons

Looking for some personal care and convenience at its finest? You can now achieve this with our on-campus barbershops and hair salons! Designed to cater to both men and women, these facilities ensure you can always maintain your style without ever stepping off campus.

Mosque and Prayer Facilities

A mosque is located next to the Deanship of Student Affairs building and is available to male students for daily prayers and religious activities. View Location

Sharjah Islamic Bank Branch and ATM

A Sharjah Islamic Bank branch and ATM services are available on the male campus, providing students with convenient access to banking and cash withdrawal services. View Location

Sharjah Cooperative Society

Grocery services are available through Sharjah Cooperative Society, located on the ground floor of the Male and Female Student Center. The store provides groceries, food products, beverages and other daily necessities.
