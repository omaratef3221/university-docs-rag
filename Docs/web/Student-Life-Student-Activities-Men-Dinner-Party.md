## Dinner Party

Under the patronage of His Excellency the Dean of Student Affairs and in the presence of the Director of the University Housing; The Housing Administration organized a dinner party for the students on Monday evening, 24-6-2024, after the Maghrib prayer in the meeting hall in the administration building; where a large number of students attended. The dinner party was preceded by a parental meeting between His Excellency the Dean, where he welcomed the students and congratulated them on the Eid after their return from the Eid Al-Adha holiday. He provided them with some guidance and instructions to help them overcome the stage they are going through.

Mr. Nasser, the Housing Director, also expressed his gratitude and appreciation to the students who responded to the invitation and provided them with the services provided by the Housing Administration for students and a glimpse of the activities organized by the administration. It was also an opportunity for the President of the Student Council to explain to the attending students the role of the council in communicating all their issues to the Deanship of Student Affairs and the university administration to work on meeting them.

The dinner party included a video presentation on the screens in the meeting hall explaining the pioneering role of the University of Sharjah in serving the local community.
