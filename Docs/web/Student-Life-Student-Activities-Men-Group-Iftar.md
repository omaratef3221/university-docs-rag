## Group Iftar

Under the direction of the Director of Housing, a group Iftar banquet was prepared on the occasion of Eid al-Adha after the Eid prayer in the hall of the Salah al-Din al-Ayyubi building. The students shared the joy of Eid. The students were very grateful for this kind gesture.
