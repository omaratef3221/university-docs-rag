## I am Creative

To celebrate the talents and creativity of students with disabilities in the artistic and cultural fields, and as a translation of the university’s vision to achieve the principle of equal opportunities for all students, the Deanship of Student Affairs at the university launched the first edition of the “Sharjah University Festival for Arts and Creativity for People with Disabilities” under the slogan “I am Creative” which was organized by the Art Studio at the Deanship.
