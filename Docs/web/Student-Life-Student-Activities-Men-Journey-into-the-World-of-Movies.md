## Journey into the World of Movies

Under the auspices of the Housing Director, the Student Housing Administration organized a recreational trip to Zero 6 Mall Cinema, where a large number of students participated in the event, reaching 53 students. Group photos were taken, and then popcorn and soft drinks were distributed to the participating students. At the end of the activity, light dinner meals were distributed to all participating students, and the students expressed their joy at the event.
