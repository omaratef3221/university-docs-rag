## Kuwait the future with its vision, based on its present, and the authenticity of its past

Organized by the Kuwaiti Cultural Club to introduce university students to the heritage, culture and civilization of the State of Kuwait and to highlight the features of the State of Kuwait, its culture, originality, civilization and nobility.

Location: Al-Razi Hall- M24
