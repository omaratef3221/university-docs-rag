## Student Clubs Exhibition

Dr. Amina Al Marzouqi, Deputy Director of the University for Student and Branch Affairs, opened the seventh annual exhibition of student clubs and scientific societies, which was organized by the Student Activities Department of the Deanship of Student Affairs at the university With the aim of providing an opportunity for cultural clubs and scientific societies and introducing the programmes, activities and events that are organized throughout the year by these clubs and societies in order to encourage students to join it.
