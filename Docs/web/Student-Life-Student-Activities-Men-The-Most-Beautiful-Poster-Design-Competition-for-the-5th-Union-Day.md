## The Most Beautiful Poster Design Competition for the 5th Union Day

The university studio at the Deanship of Student Affairs organized a competition to design the most beautiful poster, in which students from various specializations at the university participated. Aisha Tariq Youssef from the College of Science won first place, Sheikha Khaled Abdullah from the College of Engineering won second place, and Lubna Essam Krishan from the College of Communication won third place.
