## University of Sharjah Students Participate in the 20th Arab Media Forum in Kuwait

University of Sharjah Students Participate in the 20th Arab Media Forum in Kuwait A group of University of Sharjah students took part in the 20th Arab Media Forum, responding to an official invitation extended by the State of Kuwait. This participation aligns with the University’s commitment to empowering its students in the field of media and enhancing the presence of Emirati youth in regional media platforms, while proudly representing the University of Sharjah and the United Arab Emirates at a prestigious Arab media gathering. The students engaged in a variety of sessions and panel discussions that addressed globally significant topics, including artificial intelligence, digital media, and the role of media in shaping communities. The students expressed their appreciation for the opportunity, noting that it enriched their skills and provided them with valuable new experiences.
