## A LECTURE ON VOLUTEER WORK

The Student Activities Department, in collaboration with the Sharjah Volunteer Award, organized an awareness lecture on volunteer work, presented by Ms. Sheikha Al Sarkal from the award. The lecture was attended by many students and aimed to familiarize the audience with the aspects and benefits of volunteer work, and to encourage volunteering due to its societal, international, and personal benefits. Additionally, the students were introduced to the Sharjah Volunteer Award for students and encouraged to register for it.
