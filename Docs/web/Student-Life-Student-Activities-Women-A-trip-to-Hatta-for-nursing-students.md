## A Trip To Hatta For Nursing Students

The Student Activities Department in the Deanship of Student Affairs organized a recreational trip to Hatta for nursing students and their faculty members. The purpose of the trip was to provide relaxation and entertainment to break the routine in preparation for the upcoming exam period. The students enjoyed kayaking and then had a pleasant session with nature, along with some activities in Hatta Wadi Park.
