## Bag Painting Workshop

An art workshop for female students was held focusing on acrylic painting on fabric bags with the goal of enhancing artistic creativity among students and developing their skills in using acrylic paints. The workshop aimed to:

Teach Acrylic Painting Techniques: Introduce students to methods of using acrylic paints on fabrics.

Develop Artistic Skills: Improve student's abilities in drawing and coloring.

Encourage Creativity: Stimulate personal creativity and artistic expression.

Boost Self-Confidence: Build student's confidence through creating distinctive artworks.

Provide an Enjoyable and Educational Experience: Create an interactive and enjoyable environment for students.
