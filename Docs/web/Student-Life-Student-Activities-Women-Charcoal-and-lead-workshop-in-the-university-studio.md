## Charcoal And Lead Workshop In The University Studio

An art workshop for female students aimed at developing artistic skills: enhancing the student's abilities in using charcoal and pencil drawing tools.

Teaching drawing techniques: learning the basics and various techniques of charcoal and pencil drawing.

Fostering creativity: encouraging students to express their ideas and creativity through drawing.
