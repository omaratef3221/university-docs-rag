## Debate Basics Workshop (Eng)

A virtual Workshop in English on The Basics of Debating

The Deanship of Student Affairs (women), in coordination with the Department of Foreign Languages, held a virtual workshop in English titled: "The Basics of Debating". Mrs. Suhair Abu Hantash and Mrs. Dana Khalil introduced the students to: the concept of debating, the importance of providing documented evidence to support claims, and the structure of a debate. Students were also given mini tasks to complete during the workshop. This workshop is one of a series of workshops about the art of debating.
