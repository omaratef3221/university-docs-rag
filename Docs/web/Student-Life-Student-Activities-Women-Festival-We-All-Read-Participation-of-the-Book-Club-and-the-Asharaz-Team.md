## Festival 'We All Read' – Participation of the Book Club and the Asharaz Team

The Deanship of Student Affairs organized the 9th Reading Festival (We All Read), which included a book fair and various cultural activities. The Asharaz team and the Book Club participated in interactive activities and competitions related to reading, books, and authors. Prizes were awarded to the winners.
