## Student Life

## Global Village Trip

The Student Activities Department at the Deanship of Student Affairs organized a recreational trip to Global Village in Dubai, with the participation of 40 students from various university disciplines.

This trip is part of the effort to diversify experiences for students by choosing recreational venues to break the daily routine. It allows students to learn about the participating countries, their different cultures, and events, in addition to enjoying the performances on the main stage.
