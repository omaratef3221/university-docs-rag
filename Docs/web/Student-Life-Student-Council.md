## Student Council

The Students' Advisory Council is a group of students who are elected to represent UoS students. The Council helps develop a safe and supportive university environment that promotes students' services. The Council is committed to achieving its objectives in accordance with the regulations, laws, and provisions in place at UoS. Objectives

- Strengthen the spirit of cooperation and the concept of teamwork among students, and to solidify the bonds between them and the administrative staff and faculty at the university.
- Develop leadership skills among students and provide them with the opportunity to express their opinions and needs.
- Provide input regarding the various decisions issued by the university, whether those related to students, academic and administrative affairs, or decisions related to the services provided by the university.
- Contribute to solving students' academic, social, and behavioral issues in cooperation with the relevant departments at the university.
- Support the academic achievement and academic progress of university students.
- Support student activities in scientific, cultural, social, and sports fields, as well as volunteer work programs.
- Raise students' awareness of their rights and responsibilities in accordance with the regulations in force at the university.
- Submit initiatives and development proposals for plans, regulations, procedures, and activities.
Student Council Role:

- Expressing Students' Opinions on University Matters: The council serves as a platform for students to voice their perspectives and concerns regarding various aspects of university life.
- Seizing Opportunities to Better Serve Students' Interests: The council proactively seeks and utilizes opportunities to enhance the services and support provided to students, aiming to improve their overall experience.
- Defending Students' Interests and Viewpoints: The council acts as an advocate for students, representing their needs and perspectives to the university administration and relevant stakeholders.
- Launching Programs and Projects that Promote Leadership and Teamwork: The council initiates and implements programs and projects designed to develop students' leadership skills and foster a collaborative environment.
- Enhancing Student Well-being in All Aspects: The council is committed to promoting the overall well-being of students, addressing their academic, social, personal, and other needs.
- Keeping the UoS Community Informed of Important Events: The council plays a vital role in disseminating information about significant events, announcements, and updates within the university community.
