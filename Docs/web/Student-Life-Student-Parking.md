## Student Parking & Campus Access

### Student Parking Areas

Male Students

- Front parking areas of the Male Colleges
- Shaded parking areas behind Building A1
- P3 Multi-Storey Parking Building
Female Students

- Front parking areas of the Female Colleges
- Parking areas next to the Sports Complex
- Parking areas next to the Deanship of Student Affairs Building
- P1 Parking Building

### Staff & Faculty Parking

The following areas are designated for faculty and administrative staff and are not for student use:

- P2 Parking Areas
- Parking areas behind the Main Building (B2)
- Parking spaces designated for Deans and Directors (reserved exclusively; students may not use these)

### Parking Locations (Maps)

- P3 Parking Building — Males Students
- P1 Parking — Female Students
