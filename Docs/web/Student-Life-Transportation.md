## Transportation

As part of the University of Sharjah's dedication to providing quality services, we are committed to offering you utmost convenience! Our Transport Section goes the extra mile to offer top-notch transportation choices for students, with a special focus on those in dormitories.

### Transport Services for Students

With our extensive transportation services, UoS puts your convenience and safety first. We provide the following to guarantee a convenient and hassle-free commute:

- Off-Campus Training: Convenient transportation for students attending off-campus training.
- Airport Transport: Round-the-clock services to Sharjah and Dubai airports exclusively for female dormitory students.
- Weekly Shopping: Scheduled trips to various shopping centers for dormitory students.
- Emergency Transport: 24/7 emergency transportation to hospitals or medical centers for female dormitory students, as directed by Dormitory Management.
- In-Campus Shuttle: Regular bus services from 7:00 am to 5:00 pm within the campus.

### Female Students:

- From the female dormitory (W13) to the colleges: One bus every 10 minutes.
- From the nurse housing complex and medical colleges housing G to the medical and women's colleges: One bus every 20 minutes.
- Inter-campus transportation: One bus every 10 minutes between colleges. Waiting areas include W7, W3, W1, and W2 roundabouts.

### Male Students:

- From the male dormitory to the medical colleges and the College of Fine Arts and Design: One bus every hour.
- From male student waiting areas at M1 and M2 to the colleges: One bus every 15 minutes.
